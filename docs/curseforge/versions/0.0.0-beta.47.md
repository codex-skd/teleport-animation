<h2>v0.0.0-beta.47 - Fix terrain disappearing during teleport animation</h2>

<h3>Fix</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Terrain rendering during animation</strong>: The chunk section render window was frozen at the pre-teleport position, causing the ground to disappear in chunks while the transition camera moved away from the player during the ascend/descend/travel phases.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Technical Changes</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>LevelRendererMixin.teleportAnimation$maskPlayerChunkReposition()</code> — anchor <code>lastCameraSectionX/Y/Z</code> to the player position instead of the transition camera position, so vanilla's own section-change check keeps firing and repositions the <code>ViewArea</code> to the actual transition camera every frame.</li>
</ul>
