<h2>Version 1.1.6 — Opción 1 Flicker Fix</h2>

<h3>What's Fixed</h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Eliminated Transition Flicker</strong>: Removed zoom shake effect from nearby waystone slide animation. The camera now transitions smoothly from Phase 1 (ascenso) to Phase 2 (slide) without visual glitches.</li>
  <li><strong>Cleaner Animation</strong>: Slide phase now renders as pure, stable interpolation without vibration effects that were causing brief flickering at phase boundaries.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Technical Details</h3>

<ul>
  <li>Removed <code>applyZoomShake()</code> from <code>slideFrame()</code> method to eliminate flicker during nearby waystone transitions.</li>
  <li>Slide animation now returns clean <code>CameraFrame</code> with smooth easing, maintaining visual continuity from topDownFrame without shake artifacts.</li>
  <li>Shake effects remain active in other phases (pull, travel for distant waystone, push) for cinematic feel while keeping slide smooth and focused.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Impact</h3>

<p>Nearby waystone teleports (Opción 1) now feel polished and seamless, with no visual stuttering between animation phases. Long-distance teleports remain cinematic with all shake effects intact.</p>
