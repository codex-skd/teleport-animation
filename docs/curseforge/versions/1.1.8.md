<h2>v1.1.8 - Eliminate flicker in nearby and distant waystone transitions</h2>

<h3>Fix</h3>
<ul>
<li>Eliminate visual flicker in nearby waystone transitions by disabling camera shake during ascent phases</li>
<li>Remove inconsistency in distant waystone fade-in approach by smoothing the final drag without visual noise</li>
<li>Ensure Sodium terrain updates don't interfere with camera movement for seamless transitions</li>
</ul>

<h3>Details</h3>
<p>This release resolves two distinct camera transition issues that were visible when teleporting to nearby and distant waystones.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Nearby Waystones (within render distance)</strong>: Disabled visual shake in ascent phases to prevent flicker during Sodium terrain compilation; <code>slideFrame()</code> remains smooth and flicker-free</li>
<li><strong>Distant Waystones</strong>: Removed camera shake from final fade-in approach to maintain visual consistency from start to descent; eliminates the jarring transition between fade-in and descent phases</li>
</ul>

<h3>Technical</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>pullFrame()</code> and <code>topDownFrame()</code>: Shake envelope set to 0.0 for nearby waystones; preserved at 1.0 for distant waystones</li>
<li><code>prePushTopDownFrame()</code>: Fade-in drag now returns <code>CameraFrame</code> directly instead of applying shake; maintains smooth approach during screen fade-in</li>
<li>Interaction with Sodium optimized: cheap terrain updates no longer conflict with camera movement noise</li>
</ul>
