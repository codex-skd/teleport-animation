<h2>Version 1.1.5 — Opción 1: Smooth Slide Animation for Nearby Waystones</h2>

<h3>What's New</h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Nearby Waystone Slide Animation (Opción 1)</strong>: When teleporting to a waystone within render distance, the camera now smoothly slides horizontally from origin to destination in a single continuous motion.
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Phase 1 (Ascenso)</strong>: Camera rises vertically above the player.</li>
    <li><strong>Phase 2 (Teleport/Slide)</strong>: Camera slides smoothly across the landscape, interpolating XZ position while maintaining altitude.</li>
    <li><strong>Phase 3 (Descenso)</strong>: Camera descends vertically into the destination waystone.</li>
  </ul></li>
  <li><strong>Optimized for Visibility</strong>: No fade-to-black overlay — both waystones remain visible throughout the transition for maximum immersion.</li>
</ul>

<h3>How It Works</h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Automatic Detection</strong>: Triggers when destination waystone is within render distance (typically 8 chunks).</li>
  <li><strong>Smooth Interpolation</strong>: Camera position is eased between origin and destination using smooth step function.</li>
  <li><strong>Consistent Altitude</strong>: Camera maintains top-down viewing angle (pitch 90°) throughout the slide for clear sightlines.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Technical Details</h3>

<ul>
  <li>New <code>slideFrame()</code> method handles XZ interpolation for nearby teleports.</li>
  <li><code>getCameraFrame()</code> now routes to <code>slideFrame()</code> when <code>travelToNearbyWaystone</code> is true.</li>
  <li>Travel duration automatically reduced to 12-20 ticks for quick, snappy transitions.</li>
  <li>No blackout fade means nearby teleports feel immediate and responsive while long-distance teleports retain cinematic drama.</li>
</ul>
